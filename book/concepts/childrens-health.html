MAHA Roundtable — American Health & Nutrition: A Second Opinion

Children's Health & Fertility

Source: Grace Price, Alex Clark, Jillian Michaels, RFK Jr. sections

Gen Z stats (Grace Price, 18)

  • Early-onset cancer: +79% in 3 decades; obesity on track to replace smoking as #1 preventable cancer cause.
  • 1 in 5 Gen Z is obese, 1 in 6 overweight.
  • 75% of obese children have vascular age of a 45-year-old.
  • 18.7% of adolescents with NAFLD (fatty liver); ~30% pre-diabetic.
  • 1 in 5 report anxiety/depression; 1 in 7 will develop a mental-health disorder (near 0% 50 years ago).
  • 1954 Sugar Research Foundation speech (quoted): low-fat diets would shift 20% of calories to carbs → sugar consumption up by a third — "a tremendous improvement in general health."
  • $1.8M from Coca-Cola to the American Cancer Society to frame cancer as genetic, not metabolic.
  • Food pyramid: Lucky Charms ranked above ground beef (95% of the 2020 advisory committee had food/pharma COIs).

Millennial/Gen Z fertility (Alex Clark)

  • Total infertility rates up ~1%/yr (since the 1970s); sperm counts down ~1%/yr → ~50% below prior generations.
  • Harvard study: obese men have 80% higher chance of zero sperm.
  • Women: leading cause is PCOS (a metabolic-reproductive syndrome driven by high insulin → excess testosterone → anovulation; affects up to 26% of women worldwide).
  • Men: low testosterone; abdominal (midline) fat expresses aromatase, converting testosterone → estrogen ("a giant ovary").
  • Atrazine (estrogenic, illegal in other countries; ~70M lbs/yr in the US) and xenoestrogenic microplastics add estrogen load.
  • "A species-level event" — if these rates continue, American reproduction is at risk.

The generational obesity trajectory (Jillian Michaels)

  • 1974: <10% of the population overweight/obese (being obese was a circus novelty).
  • 1987: Jillian herself at 170 lb at 5'1 — clinically obese at 13.
  • 2024: ~75% of adults obese/overweight; her generation + two after = "victims of America's unchecked obesity crisis."
  • During this roundtable (~3 hrs): ~800 Americans die of an obesity-related preventable disease.
  • The quartet: Big Farming, Big Food, Big Pharma, Big Insurance — systematically corrupting institutions of trust.
  • $30B/yr in food/ beverage ads targeting kids; celebrity-appeal commercials; "anti-diet body positivity" messaging.
  • OIC (obesity drugs) linked to stomach paralysis, pancreatitis, thyroid cancer, yet surging.
  • Presidential Fitness Test (Clinton/Obama-era) should be revived — Jillian has been involved with past ones.

RFK Jr.'s fertility numbers

  • Puberty now at 10–13 (6 years earlier than 1900) — earliest on any continent.
  • Sperm counts ~50% of what they were a couple of generations ago; testosterone dropping at the same rate; breast cancer (estrogen-driven) strikes 1 in 8 women.
