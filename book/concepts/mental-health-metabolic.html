Mental Health Is a Metabolic Problem

Source: Dr. Chris Palmer & Alex Clark sections

The core claim (Dr. Chris Palmer, Brain Energy)

  • The human brain is an organ — when brain metabolism is impaired, the symptoms we call "mental illness" follow.
  • Correlation is not coincidence: as obesity/diabetes rates skyrocketed, so did mental-illness rates (all-time highs in depression, anxiety, bipolar, deaths of despair, overdoses, ADHD, autism).
  • Evidence:
    • ~300,000-person study: daily UPF eaters are 3× more likely to struggle with mental health.
    • Systematic review: UPF exposure associated with 32 health parameters, including mental-health conditions.
    • Parents with metabolic issues (obesity/diabetes) are more likely to have children with autism/ADHD — same foods/chemicals driving both.
    • Autism rates: quadrupled in 20 years (1/10,000 → 1/36 nationwide, 1/22 in California); ADHD tripled.
    • Peer-reviewed reports: ketogenic diet and dietary interventions improve severe conditions — schizophrenia and bipolar disorder, sometimes with lasting remission.
  • Underfunding: nutrition + mental-health research get <5% of the NIH budget — a "concerted effort" of industry lobbying to keep root causes unstudied.
  • Crossroads: keep managing symptoms, or fund root-cause research (foods, chemicals, medications, environmental toxins on brain and metabolism).

The Millennial angle (Alex Clark)

  • Millennials = the "uncontrolled experiment" generation: 1992 food pyramid advice, UPF engineered to be as addictive as heroin (scientists poached from the cigarette industry in the '80s/'90s).
  • Youth obesity: 5% → 15% → now ~50% of kids overweight/obese.
  • Birth-control pill era: girls put on "the magic pill" at first period (13–15) with no informed consent; infertility rising ~1%/yr; IVF costs tens of thousands when the pill is stopped.
  • Vaccine schedule: 70 shots by age 18 (27 before age 2, up to 6 in one visit) — "are we allowed to even ask?"
  • Microplastics in breastmilk; formula often contains inflammatory seed oils or soy.
