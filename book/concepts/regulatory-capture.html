MAHA Roundtable — American Health & Nutrition: A Second Opinion

Regulatory Capture

Source: RFK Jr., Callie Means closing, and Q&A

The capture pattern

  • FDA: ~75% of its funding comes from the pharma industry it regulates; FDA commissioners cycle onto pharma boards (Pfizer example).
  • NIH: 80% of grants go to people with conflicts of interest; scientists collect ~$150K/yr royalties on products they develop; 90% of NIH funding = incremental pharma R&D, not root-cause research.
  • USDA: 95% of the 2020 dietary-guidelines committee had food-industry COIs; sugar still "healthy" for 2-year-olds per the guidelines.
  • SNAP: ~10% goes to sugary drinks, ~70% to UPF — underwriting the poisoning of lower-income populations; Medicaid then absorbs the chronic-disease costs.
  • Media: ~47% of mainstream media ad revenue comes from companies that profited during the pandemic; "no-fly zone" for questioning orthodoxy.
  • Politics: pharma out-spends the oil industry 5× on donations/lobbying; 100+ members support Medicare funding for OIC ($1,500/mo) while taking money from Novo Nordisk.

Callie Means' "two simple things" (closing)

  1. Get corruption out of the science/guidelines:
    • Disallow USDA guideline writers from taking food-industry money.
    • Reset NIH's mission to foundational "why are we getting sick" research (not outsourced pharma R&D).
    • Untangle FDA from pharma funding; disaggregate the F and the D.
  2. Open up health-care flexibility:
    • Doctors can write letters of medical necessity for food/exercise → unlock HSA/HRA spending.
    • Clinically right info: heart disease ≠ statin deficiency; diabetes ≠ metformin deficiency; depression ≠ SSRI deficiency.
    • Food as medicine is the center of health care, not a "fringe" or "prevention" bucket — regenerative farming, exercise = healthcare.

RFK Jr.'s framing

  • "A broken system fueled by corruption and collusion"; Eisenhower's warning about the scientific-industrial complex (rarely-cited second half of that speech — raised by Brigham).
  • The system isn't broken, it's rigged (Brigham): "the most valuable asset is a sick child."
  • Ask: top-down: remove money from politics (Citizens United), flexible spending accounts, trust the American patient.
