# Ultra-Processed Foods (UPF)

*Source: [Callie Means, Max Lug, Grace Price sections](../sources/maha-roundtable-transcript.md)*

## Origin story (Callie Means)

- **1960s:** medical trust in antibiotics → used to sell interventionist (Flexner/Hallstead) medical education.
- **Late 1970s–80s:** smoking rates collapsed; cigarette giants (Philip Morris, RJ Reynolds) **bought the biggest food companies**; moved addiction scientists from tobacco R&D into food — engineering hyper-palatable, addictive UPF.
- **1992/2020 food pyramids:** created via captured USDA advisory committee (95% COI with big food/pharma) — a marketing document ranking Lucky Charms above ground beef; carb intake +20% in the decade after.
- **Result:** UPF = **67–73% of the modern supermarket**; **~50% of kids' diet**; every 10% more UPF → **+14% all-cause mortality, +25% Alzheimer's risk**.

## Evidence summary (Max Lug)

- Umbrella review of meta-analyses: UPF linked to **32 negative health outcomes**, **zero positive**.
- Daily UPF eaters are **3×** more likely to struggle with mental health.
- NIH-funded research: UPF drives **~800 extra kcal/day** (replicated in Japan).
- 800 calories/day of UPF → measurable mortality/morbidity risk.
- **Prevention research = 2 cents per research dollar** — resources go to treatment, not prevention.

## Gen Z angle (Grace Price)

- 1954 Sugar Research Foundation speech: a low-fat diet would shift 20% of calories to carbs, raising sugar consumption — the industry's original strategy.
- **$1.8M** Coca-Cola donation to the American Cancer Society to frame cancer as genetic, not metabolic.
- US adults eat **103% more sugar** than recommended; kids **224% more**.
- Ask: address corporate capture, not lifelong OIC prescriptions for 6-year-olds.
