## Michaela Fuller's story

- JRA at 8, hip/ankle replacements at 17, years on OxyContin and 8 medications; at 23 radical diet change → "stepped out of hell."
- After baby + medication withdrawal, symptoms returned → **beef, salt, water only** → **remission in 6 weeks**; maintained 7 years, zero meds.
- **Lion diet** (Lion Diet / Fuller Health): plant-free ketogenic diet for autoimmunity and psychiatric disorders; 500K+ social followers, 30K-person diet groups; coverage in *The Times*, Fox, Sky News Australia.
- Mechanism discussed: **intestinal permeability ("leaky gut")** — high-carb/UPF + chemicals damage the gut barrier; particles leak into blood.
- **Ask:** government-funded studies of ketogenic/plant-free ketogenic diets for autoimmunity & psychiatric disorders (only a few studies exist due to lack of funding); Crone's disease remission study; anorexia remission with dietary intervention.

## Jordan Peterson's scientific case

- **Philosophy of science:** science is an ethical/philosophical enterprise; statistics is a thousand micro-decisions, each ethical — careerists produce misleading results.
- **Radical simplification:** chronically ill people eat a multitude of foods; causal analysis needs a **single variable**. Ketogenic/plant-free-ketogenic = that simplification.
  - Keto switches the body to fat metabolism → if symptoms decline, the illness is associated with glucose metabolism.
  - **No recommended daily allowance for carbohydrate** — you can live on zero carbs (Inuit precedent: societies that survived on meat).
  - Typical obese person: **7–20 lb/month** weight loss, no hunger (keto foods are satiating); psychological benefits on depression/anxiety.
  - **Stepwise reintroduction:** resolve symptoms on the restricted diet, then add food categories one at a time to find the real culprit.
  - Verdict: "simple, elegant, implementable, necessary."
- Sen. Johnson: childhood epilepsy has been treated with ketogenic diets for a century — the brain doesn't convulse when you stop feeding it sugar.
