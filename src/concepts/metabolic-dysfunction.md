# Metabolic Dysfunction

*Source: [Dr. Casey Means & Dr. Chris Palmer sections](../sources/maha-roundtable-transcript.md)*

## The frame (Dr. Casey Means, *Good Energy*)

- **Metabolic dysfunction affects 93.2% of American adults** (American College of Cardiology).
- It's a "fundamental distortion of cellular biology" with **three in-cell processes:**
  1. **Mitochondrial dysfunction** → see [MitoQ wiki](https://iamflipse.github.io/mitoq-wiki/) for the intervention side.
  2. **Oxidative stress** — "a wildfire inside our cells."
  3. **Chronic inflammation** throughout body and gut (leaky gut / increased intestinal permeability, zulin levels).
- Largely **not genetic**: caused by toxic UPF, toxic chemicals, toxic medications, sedentary indoor lifestyles.

## "I didn't learn this in med school" facts (Stanford)

- Each extra serving of UPF: **+18% early-mortality risk**.
- **82%** of independent studies show harm from processed food; **93%** of industry-funded studies show no harm.
- **95%** of the 2020 USDA dietary-guidelines committee had food-industry conflicts of interest.
- **1 billion lbs** of synthetic pesticides/yr; 99% of US farmland sprayed.
- Microplastics: **~0.5% of brain weight** (per the panel's cited figure).
- **3,500 steps/day** average vs. **7,000 steps** slashing dementia/diabetes/cancer/obesity risk by 40–60%.
- Medical error + medications = **3rd leading cause of death**; 5 nights of sleep deprivation → pre-diabetes.
- **20%** less sleep than 100 years ago; kids get less outdoor time than max-security prisoners; adults 93% indoors (circadian biology).
- ADA/AAP take tens of millions from Coke/Cadbury/processed food and vaccine makers.

## Why it matters (Dr. Chris Palmer)

- Metabolism ≠ just calorie burning: it's the chemical-reaction series converting food into energy and building blocks.
- **Mental disorders at their core are often metabolic disorders impacting the brain** (see [mental-health-metabolic](mental-health-metabolic.md)).
- The field says complexity is why obesity can't be solved — but the cause (toxic environment) **is** easy to study; no need to map every molecular detail.
- Nutrition + mental-health research get **<5% of the NIH budget** — underfunded by industry lobbying.
- **Ask:** fund root-cause research (foods, chemicals, medications, environmental toxins on brain & metabolism).
