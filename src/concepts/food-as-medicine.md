# Food as Medicine — The Policy Asks

*Source: [closing statements (Callie Means, RFK Jr.) and Dr. McCary](../sources/maha-roundtable-transcript.md)*

## The paradigm shift

- **Prevention = 2¢ per research dollar** (Max Lug) — most resources fund treatment, not prevention.
- "We cannot treat what we refuse to diagnose" (Brigham).
- Dr. McCary: stop whack-a-mole on the back end (billing, coding, medicating); fund **food-as-medicine** research: school lunches, cooking classes, pesticide/glyphosate studies, "New Food Compass" to replace the food pyramid.
- Callie: food, exercise, and metabolic habits are **clinically** the most effective reversal treatments for chronic conditions — food as medicine must be the **center** of health care, not a "fringe" or "prevention" bucket.

## Concrete asks (Callie's "two things")

1. **Get corruption out of the science:**
   - No food-industry money for USDA guideline writers.
   - NIH: reset to root-cause ("why are we getting sick") research.
   - FDA: stop pharma funding; disaggregate food (F) and drug (D) oversight.
   - SNAP: stop subsidizing UPF (70%) for children.
2. **Open up health-care flexibility:**
   - Doctors write **letters of medical necessity** for food/exercise → unlock tax-advantaged HSA/HRA spending.
   - Give patients clinically right information; trust the American patient to choose interventions with their doctor.
   - RFK Jr.: get **Citizens United** and money out of politics; flexible spending accounts.
- **Consumer side (Jason Karp):** one coordinated 5% sales dip in a quarter forces overnight reform; buy organic/regenerative; shop the supermarket perimeter.

## The "spiritual crisis" closer (RFK Jr.)

- America went from the model democracy (1776/1792) to "the worst country in the world" on the key metric: **are we giving our children a chance to live better lives than we did?**
- We have the best regenerative/organic farmers and functional-medicine doctors, yet the worst health-care outcomes — resilience is still there; we can "take our country back."
- "Stop hating each other; start loving our children more."
- The American Revolution precedent: a small core of activists articulated new values and brought the greatest empire to its knees.
