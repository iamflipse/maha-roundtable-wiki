# Toxic Chemicals & Glyphosate

*Source: [Courtney Swan, Vonnie Hari, Jason Karp sections](../sources/maha-roundtable-transcript.md)*

## The agrochemical origin story (Courtney Swan)

- **IG Farben → IGR:** provided chemicals for Nazi nerve agents/gas chambers; later pivoted to agriculture; **Monsanto** produced Agent Orange (Vietnam); post-war they needed a market → Roundup/glyphosate.
- **1974:** glyphosate spraying begins; early 1990s: GMO "Roundup-Ready" crops let farmers spray whole fields; 2018: **Bayer buys Monsanto**.
- Today: **85–100% of US corn/soy are GMO**; 80% of GMOs are engineered to withstand glyphosate; **280 million lbs of glyphosate sprayed annually** (many imported from China).
- EWG: **80–90%** of tested wheat products (Cheerios, Goldfish, Nature Valley) contain glyphosate contamination; found in **breastmilk, placentas, organs, sperm, rain/drinking water**.
- WHO/IARC classifies glyphosate as a carcinogen; suspected link to **celiac disease/gluten sensitivity**.
- **2022 labeling law:** "made with bioengineered ingredients" — small label; glyphosate still unlabeled.
- Subsidies flow to the most pesticide-sprayed crops; those crops → HFCS + refined seed oils → UPF.
- **Ask:** stop subsidizing foods that make us sick; prioritize people over profit.
- Dr. Johnson's follow-up: **Bt corn's Bt gene** makes insect stomachs "explode" (holes in gut lining) — parallel to human "leaky gut syndrome" (cross-ref [metabolic dysfunction](metabolic-dysfunction.md)).

## One ingredient, two countries (Vonnie Hari, "Food Babe")

- **McDonald's fries:** US version **11 ingredients** (incl. dimethyl polysiloxane, formaldehyde-based foaming agent) vs **3 in the UK** (salt optional).
- **Skittles:** US version has 10 artificial dyes + titanium dioxide (banned in EU; EU requires warning labels for activity/hyperactivity in children).
- **Gatorade:** US uses Red 40 + caramel color; Germany uses carrot/sweet potato.
- **Doritos:** US has 3 artificial dyes + MSG; UK version has none.
- **Kellogg's cereal (Froot Loops):** Canada = natural colors (watermelon/blueberry/carrot); US = 4 artificial dyes + BHT (endocrine disruptor, linked to cancer); 111,000-signature petition, delivery to HQ Oct 15.
- **US: 10,000+ food additives vs EU: ~400 approved.**
- Documented petition wins: **Craft** (dyes removed after 400K signatures), **Subway** (azodicarbonamide out — the yoga-mat/shoe-rubber chemical that becomes a carcinogen when heated; $450K fine in Singapore), **Starbucks** (caramel color → UK's carrot beta-carotene; now publishes ingredients).
- "One set of ingredients over there, one set of ingredients here" — the hypocrisy of American food companies treating US consumers as the toxic version.

## Regulatory standards (Jason Karp, Human Co/Hugh Kitchen)

- **US (GRAS):** "innocent until proven guilty" — companies self-approve new chemicals; FDA rubber-stamps because it lacks staff (admitted on NPR).
- **EU:** "guilty until proven innocent" — restrict/ban for up to 10 years until proven safe.
- **Result:** Europeans live **~5 years longer**, less than half our obesity rates, better mental health, and spend ~1/3 of our per-capita health-care costs.
- **Microplastics:** NEJM plaque study — **58%** of carotid plaques contained detectable microplastics; that group had **4× mortality** over 3 years.
- **Consumer leverage:** public food companies react fast to earnings drops — a 5% sales dip from a coordinated boycott changes behavior overnight.
