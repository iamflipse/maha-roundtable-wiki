# American Health & Nutrition: A Second Opinion

**The one-line thesis of the roundtable:** America's chronic disease crisis is driven by **ultra-processed food, toxic chemicals, and corporate-captured institutions** — and it's fixable by attacking root causes (food-as-medicine) rather than symptoms.
